### Hey, I'm Pranav 👋

I'm a **designer who ships code**. I take products from a blank Figma frame to a deployed, animated, production-ready build, without the handoff gap in between.

Over the last two years I've shipped **15+ live products**: AI platforms, WebGL/3D brand sites, SaaS tools and a native Android app.

- 🔭 **Now:** Frontend Engineer & UI/UX Designer at **[Outrift Media](https://outriftmedia.com)** (since Aug 2026), building brand and commerce sites for clients across the UAE, UK and Europe
- 🏗️ **Latest ship:** **Rock1 Builders**, a construction brand site
- 🌱 **Learning:** WebGL shaders · R3F post-processing · WebGPU · agentic UX patterns
- 🤝 **Open to:** freelance 3D/interactive sites, AI product UIs, design-to-dev collaborations
- ⚡ **Fun fact:** engineering major in "Googling things effectively" 🎓
